# Mechanism checks that change drawing decisions

Apply only the error families relevant to supplied biology.

## Compartments and interfaces

- Blood lumen, endothelium, basement membrane, pericyte and glial endfoot occupy
  distinct positions. Transcytosis uses vesicles through a cell, not a giant hole
  in a junction. Luminal binding is not proof of tissue exposure or target-cell uptake.
- Put membrane-associated defenses on their appropriate interfaces; do not bury a
  soluble surface protein inside the lipid core. Distinguish mitochondrial inner
  and outer membranes when the mechanism requires it.
- A source particle, gas, released cargo and active drug may follow different
  routes. Only the supported species crosses the barrier. Keep signal, label,
  carrier and pharmacologically active drug as distinct measured objects.
- An endocytic vesicle in cytoplasm is not a nucleus. Inspect the actual endpoint.

## Molecular/reaction topology

- Distinguish oxidation state, chelation, ion capture, ROS catalysis, radical
  trapping and gene/protein replacement. These do not all mean removing all iron.
- For a specified chelator, check continuous ligand connectivity, meaningful
  stoichiometry and coordination sites against its chemistry. A conceptual motif
  is not a verified bond-level structure; use validated structures when requested.
  A printed donor count does not validate the drawing. Count the actual distinct
  donor markers and inspect whether explicit atom/bond notation has introduced
  extra atoms, detached donors or impossible valence. Do not repair that defect
  merely by labeling the same wrong structure “conceptual”.
- Separate lipid substrate, oxidized lipid and reduced product. Enlarge the actual
  strings PL-OOH and PL-OH before diagnosing a typo.
- Binding inputs converge into a product complex. Secretion and binding are
  separate steps; do not reverse a binding arrow to make a balanced layout.
- T-bars end at the inhibited target. Two inhibitions need two bar endpoints;
  an arrowhead or generic down arrow is not equivalent.

## Expression and delivery

- In a supplied eukaryotic plasmid model, nuclear transcription, mRNA export and
  cytoplasmic translation have separate locations. Delivered mRNA generally does
  not need a nuclear-transcription step; adapt for the actual organism and construct.
- Independently expressed products are separate branches from the specified
  expression process. Do not invent a common protein precursor or a regulatory
  link between co-delivered factors merely to connect the figure.
- Distinguish RNA suppression from protein binding/delivery and preserve the
  cell/assay context supported by sources.

## Evidence, time and controls

- Keep healthy-tissue physical demonstrations, disease-model findings and human
  evidence distinct. A proposed validation loop is not established clinical control.
- Differentiate pretreatment/post-event, local/arterial/venous and cell-only
  findings when these distinctions support the comparison.
  A qualifier's frame or shaded region has a scope: include only the corresponding
  experiment/row. Extending a cell-only frame to a neighboring in-vivo-supported
  route also changes the evidence story.
- Primary, secondary and exploratory analyses remain separate. Registration or
  a secondary signal is not clinical efficacy/approval.
- Check each required main-path edge and feedback; an extra feedback is not decoration.

Record each critical relation with expected observation, actual visible observation
and image region, using pass/fail/not assessable. A single unchecked “correct
mechanism” assertion is not adequate evidence.
